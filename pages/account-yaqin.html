Membership tiers

Member: $0–149.99 · VIP1: $150–499.99 · VIP2: $500–1,499.99 · VIP3: $1,500+. Eligible spend is calculated over the latest 12 months.

2

Earning and releasing points

Earn 1, 1.1, 1.2 or 1.3 points per eligible $1 based on tier. Points become available 30 days after shipment and refund checks.

3

Points rewards

Redeem 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000 points for $5 / $10 / $20 / $30 rewards with minimum spends of $100 / $200 / $400 / $600.

4

Discount combinations

Points rewards may combine with your base member discount. They do not combine with quantity discounts, first-order offers, creator codes or temporary promotions.

5

Validity and refunds

Points expire 12 months after becoming available. Returns recalculate points and tier status; points rewards cannot be withdrawn as cash.
